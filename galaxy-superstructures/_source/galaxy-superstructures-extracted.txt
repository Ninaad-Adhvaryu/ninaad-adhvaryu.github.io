SOURCE EXTRACT — "How robustly can clustering algorithms recover cosmic superstructures from
X-ray galaxy cluster catalogues? A method comparison under input sparsity"
Pulled from Google Drive (fileId 17EYLq-W3lY__H3k7vE_DD871kD5YfHOf, PDF) via read_file_content, 2026-07-04.
Student author (British School New Delhi); keep anonymous per house rule. No institution names in essay.

CORE QUESTION
Cosmic superstructures = coherent overdensities of galaxy clusters spanning hundreds of Mpc.
Quipu (Böhringer et al. 2025): a 428 Mpc filament of 68 X-ray clusters, "largest known cosmic
structure," ~2.4e17 solar masses, found by friends-of-friends (FoF) on the CLASSIX catalogue with a
redshift-scaled linking length. Question: does such a result HOLD under a different clustering
algorithm, or with a SPARSER input catalogue? Not previously tested systematically.

METHOD
Six clustering methods on 317 X-ray clusters (MCXC catalogue, redshift slice z=0.03-0.07), scored
against the 185 published superstructure members by binary F1. Robustness = random subsampling.
Second slice z=0.04-0.08 (324 clusters) tests slice-dependence.
Methods: (1) FoF fixed linking length L=50 Mpc; (2) FoF redshift-scaled linking length
l(z)=(2 N_Cl)^(-1/3); (3) DBSCAN (eps=50 Mpc, min_samples=3); (4) HDBSCAN (min_cluster_size=10,
min_samples=3); (5) single-linkage hierarchical (cut at 50 Mpc); (6) Gaussian Mixture Model (K=10).
Positions: equatorial->comoving Cartesian, flat LCDM H0=70, Om=0.3. Ground truth from Böhringer
Table A.1. Off-the-shelf (UNOPTIMISED) hyperparameters on purpose. Metrics: adjusted Rand index,
binary F1, per-superstructure Jaccard. Sparsity sweep: 100 bootstraps at fractions {1,0.8,0.6,0.4,0.2}.
Whole thing runs in ~30 min on a laptop CPU. Code on Zenodo, fully reproducible from 2 VizieR catalogues.

THE PIVOT (what "misbehaves")
The method that WINS at full density is the WORST under sparsity — it falls off a cliff to F1=0.00.
- Full sample: FoF-fixed = DBSCAN = single-linkage all tie at highest F1 = 0.74 (prec 0.62, rec 0.91).
  FoF-fixed and single-linkage are mathematically IDENTICAL (ARI=1.000). DBSCAN agrees on binary F1.
  FoF redshift-scaled 0.67; GMM 0.50 (over-grouping: prec 0.34, rec 0.96); HDBSCAN 0.47 (prec 0.37, rec 0.64).
- Sparsity (primary slice): fixed-scale methods 0.74(100%) -> 0.61(80%) -> 0.00(60%). CLIFF.
  HDBSCAN: 0.58(80%), 0.50(60%), 0.49(40%), 0.41(20%) — holds F1>=0.30 across the whole range.
  Redshift-scaled FoF: 0.49(60%), 0.33(40%), 0.00(20%). GMM: 0.47(60%), 0.05(40%), 0.00(20%).
- Sensitivity slice (z=0.04-0.08): fixed-scale 0.65 full, cliff at 80%. HDBSCAN 0.31-0.39 across all.
  HDBSCAN is the ONLY method with median F1>0.30 at the 20% subsample in BOTH slices.
=> Trade-off: full-density accuracy (fixed-scale) vs robustness under input sparsity (adaptive-density).

MECHANISM (why the cliff — it's percolation)
Fixed linking length L=50 Mpc vs mean inter-cluster separation ~67 Mpc at full density
(comoving volume ~9.5e7 Mpc^3). Subsampling grows mean separation by (fraction)^(-1/3); at 60%
it's ~80 Mpc, well ABOVE L, so the superstructure fragments into pieces below the 20-member
threshold -> F1 collapses. Bootstrap 16-84% bands widen most at the cliff, consistent with a
PERCOLATION transition in the FoF connectivity graph. HDBSCAN uses relative density contrast (no
fixed length scale), so it still finds dense cores when the sample thins — at the cost of lower
full-sample F1 (it excludes the periphery that single-linkage chains in).

OTHER NUMBERS / TEXTURE
- Per-superstructure Jaccard (FoF fixed): Shapley 0.83, Sculptor-Pegasus 0.76, Quipu 0.55,
  Hercules 0.23, Serpens-Corona Borealis 0.13. Serpens-CrB + Hercules MERGE into one 42-cluster
  component at 50 Mpc (projection overlap; a known single-linkage failure mode).
- Quipu: only 35 of 68 published members are even in MCXC (33 northern members live in deeper
  CLASSIX/REFLEX II/NORAS II, absent from MCXC) -> unrecoverable at any skill. Recovered candidate:
  30 true positives, 20 false positives, 38 not recovered.
- GMM over-groups (fits ellipsoidal Gaussians to filamentary structure — wrong tool).

HONEST CAVEAT / SCOPE
Descriptive comparison at fixed catalogue + fixed hyperparameters; NO causal ranking, NO new
structure discovered. MCXC subset (317) shallower than CLASSIX (345). Off-the-shelf hyperparameters
(tuning would shift absolute F1 but not the qualitative cliff, per the density argument). Positions
only (no mass/luminosity/redshift-space). Bootstrap uncertainties omit ground-truth systematics.
Two slices of one catalogue — don't extrapolate to other surveys. Relevance: eROSITA all-sky survey
has heterogeneous depth -> cross-check fixed-scale FoF against HDBSCAN where completeness < ~80%.
