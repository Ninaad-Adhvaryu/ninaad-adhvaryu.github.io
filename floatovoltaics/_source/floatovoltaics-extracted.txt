Energy Generation, Net Deliverable Yield, and Evaporative Water Conservation"

CORE
Deterministic spatial model of floating solar PV (FPV) at a fixed 10% surface coverage across 10 major
California reservoirs (>1000 ha). Inputs: point-specific PVOUT (Global Solar Atlas, kWh/kWp/yr) +
evaporation depth (NASA POWER). Outputs: gross annual energy, NET deliverable energy (linear transmission
loss 0.03%/km to nearest urban load center >=80k pop), volumetric water savings (evaporation reduction
20-60% band, 40% midpoint), and a DUAL-BENEFIT efficiency metric kWh/ML (energy per megalitre of water saved).
DUAL BENEFIT: panels make power AND save water by shading the surface -> less evaporation. Turns passive
water infrastructure into dual-purpose utilities.

MODEL / PARAMS
Egross = A_ha * 0.10(coverage) * 1.5(MWp/ha) * PVOUT * 0.80(PR) * 1000 / 1e6  [GWh/yr]
Wsaved = A_ha * 0.10 * devap(m/yr) * rreduction(0.40 midpoint) * 10  [ML/yr]  (1 m over 1 ha = 10 ML)
Net = gross * (1 - 0.0003 * dist_km).  10% coverage preserves shoreline/recreation buffers.
Reservoirs (area ha / PVOUT kWh/kWp/yr / evap m/yr / nearest city @ km):
 Shasta 12140.6/1521/1.28/Redding 35;  Oroville 6398.1/1638/1.42/Sacramento 121;
 San Luis 5139.5/1812/1.68/San Jose 84; Folsom 3716/1654/1.45/Sacramento 37;
 Berryessa 8094/1683/1.38/Napa 42;      Trinity 5584/1479/1.22/Redding 91;
 New Melones 4856/1724/1.55/Stockton 93; Don Pedro 3360/1741/1.58/Modesto 59;
 Almanor 10521/1548/1.31/Chico 103;      Millerton 1640/1853/1.74/Fresno 39.

KEY NUMBERS
- Combined gross energy 11,979.9 GWh/yr; combined midpoint water conservation 34,441.0 ML/yr.
- Gross energy range: Shasta HIGHEST 2,215.9 GWh/yr (biggest lake); Millerton lowest 364.7. Driven by
  SURFACE AREA (order-of-magnitude range), NOT solar yield.
- Water savings: Shasta highest 6,216.0 ML/yr; Millerton lowest 1,141.4.
- Dual-benefit efficiency (kWh/ML, 40% midpoint): LAKE BERRYESSA HIGHEST 365,870 kWh/ML; Millerton lowest
  319,483. Berryessa wins because it has LOW ambient evaporation depth per hectare -> more electricity per
  unit water preserved. Efficiency is INVERSELY related to evaporation depth.
- Transmission: average net loss 2.11%; Oroville WORST 3.63% (121 km from Sacramento) = ~45.6 GWh/yr lost.
  <50 km -> <1.5% loss; >100 km -> ~3%+ loss.
- Pareto (energy vs water): SHASTA is the sole strictly Pareto-optimal site (dominates both, via scale +
  solar). But Berryessa wins the efficiency RATIO. So "biggest" (Shasta) != "most efficient" (Berryessa).

THE PIVOTS (the interesting moves)
1. The METRIC decides the winner. Gross energy -> Shasta (biggest). Energy-per-water (kWh/ML) -> Berryessa.
   The biggest producer is NOT the most efficient dual-purpose site. Change the yardstick, change the answer.
2. Gross vs NET (transmission) RE-RANKS the mid/low tier: Folsom climbs past Oroville (37 vs 121 km to
   Sacramento shields it); Don Pedro past New Melones; Millerton past San Luis. Remoteness "quietly
   degrades" real-world value. A site's worth depends on distance to the load, not just generation.
3. Reframe the benchmark: from "energy per dollar" (favors big land-hungry desert arrays) to "energy per
   unit of water saved" -> surfaces a DIFFERENT priority set for water-stressed regions. Lake Berryessa
   retained #1 in BOTH gross and net efficiency frameworks (net 361,259.6 kWh/ML); San Luis dropped to
   bottom in the net framework (84 km to San Jose).

HONEST CAVEAT / SCOPE
It's a MODEL, not a measurement. Deterministic; annualized CLIMATOLOGY averages (omit seasonal solar,
temperature spikes, and drought drawdowns that shrink reservoirs -> change FPV footprint + evaporation
physics). Evaporation reduction 20-60% is a WIDE uncertainty band needing site-specific micrometeorology
before contracts. Linear transmission penalty ignores grid capacity / substation interconnect constraints.
Line-of-sight distances. Fixed 10% coverage. The value is the FRAMEWORK + the metric-dependence insight,
not precise siting. Recommendations: prioritise kWh/ML over gross capacity; sites <50 km priority
(<1.5% loss); >100 km weigh against dedicated transmission cost; treat 20-60% evap band as uncertainty.
